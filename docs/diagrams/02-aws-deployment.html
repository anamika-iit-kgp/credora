Credora · AWS deployment

One region, one VPC from the Exasol launcher, two EC2 hosts and three RDS databases. Ports and protocols on every path. Numbers give the order.

Two domains, one host

  • • credora.loan is the bank, with the app at the root, a www redirect and docs., video. and ppt.
  • • credora.link serves 3rd party data via API, with a landing page and the cibil., gst. and aa. relays
  • • Every record points at the app host's Elastic IP. Caddy gets the TLS certificates

Narrow doors

  • • Only 80 and 443 are open to the world, on the app host
  • • Exasol 22, 2581, 8443, 8563 and app-host SSH are open to admin IPs only
  • • RDS is private, with 1521, 3306 and 5432 reachable from inside the VPC only

Portable by design

  • • OpenTofu (run in Docker) adds subnets, RDS, app host, S3, secret and DNS to the launcher's VPC
  • • No account IDs, IPs or domains in code. They come from the env file or are looked up at deploy time
  • • Secrets are generated per account into Secrets Manager and read with the instance role
