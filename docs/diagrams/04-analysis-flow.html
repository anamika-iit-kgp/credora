One call, thirteen logged steps

  • • Steps 1–2 read the bank's databases in place through the three virtual schemas
  • • Steps 3–6 fetch 3rd party data from CIBIL, GSTN and AA via API, from Python UDFs in 16 batches
  • • Every step writes its rows and milliseconds to RUN_LOG

Model, then policy

  • • Step 8 uses the active model from BucketFS and trains v1 if none exists
  • • Step 9 scores PD with per-feature attribution in 1.9 s
  • • In step 10 an Isolation Forest flags the most unusual 3% (circular trading, inflated turnover) in 1.3 s
  • • Step 11 prices the offer and the negotiation limit from CONFIG. RAROC must meet the hurdle, and a flagged approve becomes refer, never decline
  • • Step 13 scores the challenger scorecard in the shadow. It counts the decisions it would change and changes none

From skyline to the bank's CRM

  • • PREFERRING HIGH RAROC_OFFER PLUS LOW PD PLUS HIGH OFFER_AMOUNT peels skyline layers
  • • Layers are peeled until 50 approved companies are ranked. Those are the RMs' top 50
  • • After the run the backend upserts crm.offer, and negotiation steps go to crm.offer_event
